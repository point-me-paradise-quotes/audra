How we work
We don't book hotels. We build trips. Custom guides, trip app, real human when something goes sideways. The work you'd hate doing — already done.

You land at Montego Bay at 4:12 p.m. on a Sunday. Jamaica sits on Eastern Standard Time year-round and doesn't observe daylight saving, which means in October the clock reads exactly the same as Dallas — no time change, no adjustment, nothing to recalculate.

From the airport, both resorts are east along the A1 coast road. Runaway Bay is roughly an hour and fifteen minutes; Tower Isle is closer to an hour and forty-five. Add about an hour for immigration and baggage, and you're at dinner either way — but that half-hour difference reappears on departure day, and it matters more then. More on that below.

Departure day is the early one

A 12:18 p.m. departure is a morning flight by the time you account for the drive. Montego Bay asks international passengers to arrive three hours ahead, and MBJ genuinely earns that on a Friday.

  • From Couples Tower Isle: plan to leave around 7:30 a.m. — roughly 1 hr 45 min on the road, at the airport by 9:15.
  • From Bahia Principe Runaway Bay: plan to leave around 8:00 a.m. — roughly 1 hr 15 min, at the airport by 9:15.

Either way, breakfast is early and the last morning isn't a beach morning. If that's a real disappointment, tell me now and I'll look at a later return — there are afternoon options out of MBJ, and it's a much easier conversation before you deposit than after.

Option A — Couples Tower IsleOcho Rios · Deluxe Garden · $3,442.77

Tower Isle sits on its own stretch of sand about ten minutes east of Ocho Rios proper — close enough to reach Dunn's River Falls or the Blue Hole in twenty minutes, far enough that the cruise-ship crowd never washes up on your beach. It's the oldest of the four Couples properties and the smallest-feeling, which is the point.

The premise

Couples Resorts is exactly what the name says: the brand requires two people traveling together. That single rule shapes everything downstream. There are no children, no singles rate, no groups, and no timeshare desk in the lobby. There is also no tipping — not discouraged, not permitted. Nobody at the bar is working an angle, because there's no angle to work.

What's included that usually isn't

  • Scuba diving, water skiing, and paddleboarding — included, subject to availability. Diving normally requires certification, so if you're not certified, tell me and I'll confirm what a resort course would cost.
  • Airport transfers both ways, arranged by the resort. No separate line item, no shared shuttle waiting on a full van.
  • The private island. A short boat ride offshore, with its own bar and grill. Sunbathing there is clothing-optional — worth knowing in advance, and equally worth knowing that it's entirely separate from the main beach.
  • Jamaica's tourism tax, $4 per room per night, is already inside the rate.

How the price got here

Couples is running a fifth-night-free promotion on new bookings for travel through October 31, 2026, with a four-night minimum. Your five nights qualify, and the free night is already reflected in the $3,442.77 — there's no separate credit to claim at check-in.

Two things to know before you commit: the complimentary catamaran tour from Tower Isle was discontinued as of April 1 and is no longer available — you'll still see it referenced in older reviews, so I'd rather you hear it from me. And name changes are not permitted on this booking, which makes getting the passport names exactly right on the first pass more important here than usual.
Option B — Bahia Principe Escape Runaway BayRunaway Bay · Junior Suite Deluxe · $3,146.42

Runaway Bay sits about halfway between Montego Bay and Ocho Rios — quieter than either, with a long beach and not much town to speak of. The Escape property is the adults-only building inside Bahia Principe's Jamaica complex, now operating under the Hyatt Inclusive Collection banner, with a second Bahia resort next door that you also have the run of.

What the all-inclusive covers

  • All meals and beverages, including 24-hour room service.
  • International and premium liquors, plus unlimited drinks at every bar.
  • Unlimited à la carte dining in the specialty restaurants — semi-formal attire required, so pack accordingly.
  • Beach and pool bar service reserved for hotel guests.
  • Daily activities and evening entertainment.
  • Free 24-hour Wi-Fi throughout.
  • All local taxes and gratuities, included in the rate.
  • Turndown service.

Your booking perks

  • A “Welcome Home” door banner on arrival.
  • One bottle of wine, rum, or a local spirit in the room.
  • 25% off a spa service — one per person, per stay.
  • 10% off every themed dinner, with no limit on how often you use it. That covers the romantic dinners, lobster nights, and tomahawk dinners.

Next door at Bahia Principe Explore Jamaica

Your stay includes access to all facilities, the buffet, and the à la carte restaurants at the neighboring Explore property — no transportation needed. It roughly doubles your dining options for a week where you'll eat fifteen meals on property.

Plan around this: specialty restaurants require reservations, made through the resort app, and the app is where availability lives. Book the first few nights before you fly. Arriving Sunday evening and hoping to walk into a specialty restaurant is how people end up at the buffet on night one.
