You land at Los Cabos at 2:22 p.m. on a Sunday, clear immigration, and get met for the drive west along the corridor to Cabo San Lucas — about 27 miles, roughly 45 minutes to an hour depending on traffic through San José. By late afternoon you're on Médano, which is the one beach in Cabo San Lucas where the water is genuinely calm enough to swim. That is not true of most of the coastline here, and it's the single biggest reason this address is worth what it costs.

Cabo runs on Mountain Standard Time year-round and does not observe daylight saving, so while you're there you'll be two hours behind Dallas. October is one of the better windows — the water is at its warmest after a summer of heating, humidity is starting to break, and the high-season crowds and rates haven't landed yet.

Where You're Staying

Riu Palace Baja California

Adults only, 24-hour all-inclusive, and sitting directly on the sand at El Médano.

The Palace Baja California opened in December 2018 and is the newest of the three Riu properties clustered on Médano Beach. It's the adults-only one — 18 and over throughout, no water park, no kids' club, no families at the pool bar. Riu Santa Fe is next door and Riu Palace Cabo San Lucas sits a short walk down the same beach; you have access to both, which matters more than it sounds like it does.

The building is 602 suites arranged around four pools, two of them with swim-up bars. Renova Spa is on property. So is the gym, and tennis and pickleball courts. The tone is social rather than hushed — there's live music, a nightly entertainment program, and the Riu Party at Santa Fe on rotating nights. If you want silence, you book the far end of the beach. If you want a resort where something is always happening and you can opt in or out of it, this is the right building.

Before You Go

Things worth knowing now

The details that are easier to plan around than to discover on arrival.

Médano Beach and what's around itLocation, distances, and where to walk
  • Médano is swimmable. Most of the Los Cabos coastline is not — the Pacific side and much of the corridor have undertow that makes swimming unsafe. Médano sits inside the bay, protected by the headland at Land's End, and the water is calm. This is the practical reason to be on this beach.
  • The marina is about 4.5 km from the resort — a short taxi ride, or a walk along the beach if you're not in a hurry. That's where you'll find restaurants, shops, and the boats out to El Arco.
  • The beach is public and it's active. Vendors, jet skis, parasailing, beach clubs. Some people love it; some people want quiet. It's worth knowing which one you are before you arrive.
  • El Arco at Land's End is a 20-minute water taxi from the marina. Go in the morning — the light is better and the water is flatter.
  • Los Cabos airport is 27 miles east, on the San José del Cabo side. Your transfers are included in both directions.
Resort policies and advisoriesStraight from the hotel
  • Adults only, and enforced. Student groups and spring breakers — as groups or as individuals, regardless of age — are not accepted at any time of year. Guests generating complaints can be removed at their own expense. In-room parties are not permitted.
  • All-inclusive wristbands are required for identification throughout your stay.
  • The Taj Mahal restaurant is closed. Riu discontinued it in November 2023, so disregard it if you see it referenced in older reviews or listings.
  • Environmental sanitation tax: 83 pesos per room, per night, collected at the hotel and subject to change without notice.
  • Health and entry protocols can change by destination and by travel date. I monitor this and will confirm what applies closer to departure.
